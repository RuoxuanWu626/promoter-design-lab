Mode 2 · What interactions do the models learn?
Four matched constructs — background, A only, B only, A+B — give the position-wise residual I = f(A+B) − f(A) − f(B) + f(bg). It measures departure from additivity in the model, on the scale the model emits. It is not evidence of biological cooperation.
